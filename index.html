01 Hospitality Operations Live Product

Tableline

Staff scheduling and availability platform for multi-unit restaurant operators.

Restaurant scheduling fails when tools are either too basic or hopelessly complex. Tableline gives general managers a focused weekly shift orchestrator across Floor, Kitchen, Bar, and Open shifts, with direct schedule delivery to team inboxes and tenant billing.

Key Capabilities
  • Multi-location, multi-tenant workspace isolation
  • Shift roles: Floor, Kitchen, Bar, and Open assignments
  • Automated email delivery of schedules to staff
  • Integrated Stripe billing with tier management

02 Commerce & Foodservice Live Product

CateringHero

Turnkey catering menu builder and ordering workflows for Shopify merchants.

Catering sales involve headcounts, packaging minimums, per-person pricing, and custom lead-time rules that out-of-the-box Shopify themes cannot handle. CateringHero enables commercial kitchens and restaurants to sell catering directly on their existing store.

Key Capabilities
  • Modular catering menu and platter builder
  • Direct checkout integration on native Shopify pipelines
  • Guest count thresholds and lead-time constraints
  • Merchant fulfillment limits and blackout calendars

03 Financial Automation Live Product

VendorGhosted

Autonomous invoice chasing and escalation workflows for independent contractors.

Independent contractors and boutique agencies lose revenue because chasing unpaid invoices manually is uncomfortable and time-consuming. VendorGhosted links to personal Gmail accounts via OAuth to draft and dispatch calibrated follow-ups on scheduled escalation curves.

Key Capabilities
  • Personal Gmail OAuth (sends authentic emails from user's address)
  • Calibrated tone-matching tailored to client relationship
  • Configurable escalation curves with automatic reply detection
  • Resilient job scheduling using BullMQ and Redis queues
Next.js Gmail API BullMQ Redis TypeScript

05 Mobility & Clean Tech Live Product

AskZoe

National electric vehicle discovery and incentive matching guide for Canada.

Navigating EV adoption in Canada involves complex provincial rebate rules, cold-weather range penalties, and home charging calculations. AskZoe indexes 126+ EV models across 36 manufacturers, delivering real-world Canadian driving economics. Powered by Kord Mobility.

Key Capabilities
  • Comprehensive catalog of 126 EV models and trims
  • Sub-zero winter range and charging speed calculations
  • Federal and provincial rebate eligibility matcher
  • Side-by-side total cost per kilometer comparison model
Next.js TypeScript Pipelines Kord Mobility

06 Regulatory Compliance In Development

Madeproof

FTC "Made in USA" substantiation ledger and component traceability engine.

The Federal Trade Commission enforces strict domestic manufacturing standards. Madeproof gives manufacturers a structured system to trace bill-of-materials components back to verified supplier affidavits, creating tamper-evident cryptographic audit packets.

Key Capabilities
  • Bill of materials (BOM) multi-tier component mapping
  • Supplier affidavit collection and expiry tracking
  • Cryptographically signed SHA-256 audit packet exports
  • SKU-level compliance status inheritance
Next.js Prisma PostgreSQL SHA-256

02 Operating Thesis

Studio Standards

We operate as an independent digital foundry. No bloated venture roadmaps, no speculative features — only software that directly serves the operator.

01

Operational Clarity

We design software for real people in high-friction environments — the shift manager on an iPad behind the bar, the caterer prepping deliveries, or the founder verifying supply chains. Usability is an operational imperative.
